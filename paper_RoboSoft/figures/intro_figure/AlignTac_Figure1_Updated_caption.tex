\textbf{From labeling bottlenecks to aligned geometric supervision.}
    (a) Real-contact collection pairs a tactile image with mesh geometry at a registered pose, requiring registration for each labeled press.
    (b) Simulation provides geometric targets, while tactile appearance requires sensor-specific calibration or a learned renderer; PBR and learned renderings illustrate the appearance gap.
    (c) AlignTac uses a small registered set to align known geometry with unlabeled real images and produce geometric pseudo-labels.
    The initial and aligned depth maps show the same contact as in (a,b); the bottom row shows RGB--aligned-depth pairs for additional objects.
    Solid arrows show data flow; the \FinalLine{dashed purple arrow indicates training support.}

\caption{\textbf{From registered labels to aligned geometric supervision.}
(a) A real contact is labeled by projecting the mesh at the state $q^\ast$ read from the robot, which requires calibration and verification and must be redone after re-placing the object (right).
(b) Simulation gives labels without registration, but the tactile RGB needs a sensor-response model: PBR renders the simulated scene, and a learned renderer maps the depth label to RGB after fitting on registered real pairs.
(c) AlignTac keeps the real image and aligns the known mesh to it with an aligner trained on $K$ registered contacts, yielding pseudo-labels for the remaining real images.
Outlines mark the mesh contact region.}
